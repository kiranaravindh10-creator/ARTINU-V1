Curates Website Development & Enhancement Requirements (Based on ArtinU)

Objective

Develop Curates by taking inspiration from the existing ArtinU platform while significantly improving the UI/UX, system architecture, business workflow, scalability, and overall professionalism.

The final website should resemble a premium commercial product rather than an AI-generated template. It should deliver a modern, responsive, smooth, and highly interactive experience similar to leading consumer platforms.


---

1. Overall Website Design

High Priority

Redesign the complete UI to eliminate the "AI-generated template" appearance.

Improve spacing, typography, consistency, animations, and alignment.

Use a premium visual language similar to flagship technology brands.

Every page should feel handcrafted rather than template-based.

Improve responsiveness for mobile, tablet, and desktop.

Introduce subtle transitions and smooth scrolling.

Maintain consistent branding throughout the website.

Add an official Curates logo across the site.

First impression should immediately communicate professionalism and trust.

23. UI/UX Expectations

The final website should feel comparable to premium platforms in terms of polish and user experience. It should emphasize smooth animations, responsive layouts, fast loading, clean typography, intuitive navigation, and realistic imagery. Avoid generic AI-style illustrations or template-like sections. Every interaction should feel refined and production-ready.
